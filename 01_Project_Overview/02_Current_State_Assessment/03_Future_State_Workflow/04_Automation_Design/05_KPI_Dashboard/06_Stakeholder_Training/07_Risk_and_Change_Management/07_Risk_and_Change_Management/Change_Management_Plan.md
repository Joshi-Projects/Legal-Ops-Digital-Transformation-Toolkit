# Change Management Plan

## Purpose

This document explains how stakeholders will be guided through the adoption of a new digital legal operations workflow.

The goal is to make sure business users, legal teams, IT, and operations stakeholders understand the new process, use the legal self-service workflow correctly, and support continuous improvement.

## Change Objective

To support adoption of a structured legal self-service and workflow automation process across business teams.

## Why Change Is Needed

The current legal request process depends heavily on emails, chat messages, and manual follow-ups. This creates delays, limited visibility, and inconsistent prioritization.

A digital legal operations workflow can improve:

- Request transparency
- Legal workload visibility
- Response time
- SLA tracking
- Stakeholder experience
- Process standardization
- KPI reporting

## Key Stakeholders

| Stakeholder Group | Role in Change |
|---|---|
| Legal Team | Owns legal service delivery and validates workflow rules |
| Business Teams | Submit legal requests through the new intake process |
| IT Team | Supports technical setup and system integration |
| Operations Team | Supports process improvement and adoption |
| Management | Reviews KPIs and removes blockers |

## Change Activities

| Activity | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Stakeholder mapping | Identify affected teams and users |
| Pilot launch | Test the workflow with a small business unit |
| Training session | Explain how to use the legal request form |
| Feedback collection | Improve workflow before wider rollout |
| Communication plan | Explain benefits and new process expectations |
| KPI review | Monitor adoption, SLA performance, and request quality |

## Adoption Strategy

1. Start with one business unit as a pilot
2. Collect feedback from users and legal team
3. Improve the request form and routing logic
4. Launch to wider business teams
5. Monitor adoption through dashboard KPIs
6. Continue improving based on usage data and stakeholder feedback

## Communication Plan

| Audience | Message | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| Business Teams | Use the new legal request form instead of email | Teams announcement / email |
| Legal Team | New workflow improves request visibility and prioritization | Team meeting |
| IT Team | Support required for workflow and automation setup | Project sync |
| Management | Dashboard provides better service transparency | Monthly reporting |

## Success Indicators

| Indicator | Target |
|---|---|
| Requests submitted through digital intake | 80%+ after rollout |
| Incomplete requests | Reduced by 30% |
| Email-based requests | Reduced by 40% |
| Stakeholder satisfaction | Improved through easier request process |
| SLA tracking | Visible through dashboard |
