# Project Charter: Legal Operations Self-Service Transformation

## Project Title
Legal Operations Self-Service and Workflow Automation Project

## Business Problem
Legal teams often receive business requests through email, chat, and informal channels. This creates limited visibility, inconsistent prioritization, duplicated follow-ups, and difficulty measuring legal service performance.

## Project Goal
To design a scalable legal operations workflow that enables business teams to submit legal requests through a structured digital intake form, routes requests automatically, tracks progress, and provides KPI visibility for legal leadership.

## Scope

### In Scope
- Legal request intake form
- Request categorization
- Priority classification
- Automated routing logic
- KPI dashboard mockup
- Stakeholder training guide
- Risk and change management plan

### Out of Scope
- Real legal advice
- Real company data
- Production deployment
- Confidential legal documents

## Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role |
|---|---|
| Legal Team | Process owner and service provider |
| Business Teams | Submit legal requests |
| IT Team | Supports technical workflow implementation |
| Operations Team | Supports process improvement and adoption |
| Management | Reviews KPI performance and efficiency |

## Success Metrics

| KPI | Target |
|---|---|
| Legal request intake standardization | 100% through digital form |
| Manual email follow-ups | Reduced by 40% |
| Average request turnaround time | Reduced by 25% |
| Request status visibility | Improved through dashboard |
| Stakeholder satisfaction | Improved through training and self-service |
