# Legal Tech User Guide

This guide explains how internal business users can submit legal requests through the digital self-service process.

## Purpose

This guide helps internal business users understand how to submit legal requests through the digital legal self-service process.

## When to Submit a Legal Request

Submit a request when you need support with:

- Contract review
- Vendor agreement review
- Data protection or GDPR questions
- Compliance policy clarification
- Debt collection process-related legal questions
- Customer-facing legal communication
- Business process legal support

## How to Submit a Request

1. Open the Legal Self-Service Portal
2. Select the correct request type
3. Complete all mandatory fields
4. Upload supporting documents
5. Select urgency level
6. Submit the request
7. Track status through automated updates

## Best Practices

- Provide clear business context
- Attach all relevant documents
- Avoid sending duplicate requests by email or chat
- Use the knowledge base for general questions
- Escalate only when there is real urgency

## Expected Response Times

| Priority | Expected Response |
|---|---|
| High | 1-2 business days |
| Medium | 3-5 business days |
| Low | 5-7 business days |

## What Happens After Submission

1. The request is automatically stored in the legal request tracker
2. The request is categorized by type
3. The correct legal queue receives a notification
4. The requester receives confirmation
5. Status updates are sent during the process
6. The request is closed and included in KPI reporting
