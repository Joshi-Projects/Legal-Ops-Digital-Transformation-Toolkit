# Risk Register

This document identifies risks related to legal operations workflow implementation.

## Purpose

This risk register identifies possible risks in implementing a digital legal operations workflow and suggests mitigation actions.

## Risk Register Table

| ID | Risk | Impact | Probability | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Business users continue sending requests by email or chat | High | Medium | Provide training and clear communication about the new process |
| R2 | Legal intake form is too complex | Medium | Medium | Apply UX and legal design principles to simplify the form |
| R3 | Legal team does not fully adopt the new workflow | High | Medium | Involve legal stakeholders early during workflow design |
| R4 | Automation routes requests incorrectly | High | Low | Test routing rules before launch and create manual override option |
| R5 | Dashboard data is incomplete or inaccurate | Medium | Medium | Define mandatory fields and perform data quality checks |
| R6 | Stakeholders misunderstand self-service limits | Medium | Medium | Add clear guidance, disclaimers, and escalation path |
| R7 | Sensitive data is submitted incorrectly | High | Medium | Include privacy guidance and access controls |
| R8 | SLA reminders create notification overload | Medium | Low | Configure reminders only for relevant deadlines and overdue requests |

## Risk Management Approach

The project uses a preventive risk approach by identifying adoption, data quality, automation, and stakeholder communication risks early.
